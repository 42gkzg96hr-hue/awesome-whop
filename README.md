## Whop in numbers

Rounded public estimates, useful to set realistic expectations:

| Metric | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Cumulative sales through Whop | ~$2.67B (Feb 2026) | Sacra estimates |
| Monthly sales volume | ~$80M (Dec 2024), approaching $100M (spring 2025) | Sacra estimates |
| Sellers | ~183,600 | Sacra estimates |
| Discover monthly visitors | ~4M | Sacra estimates |
| Products listed | ~400,000 | WhopTrends |
| Products earning anything | 8 to 11% | WhopTrends |
| Median earning product | $72 to $97 / month | WhopTrends |
| Products above $10k / month | ~961 | WhopTrends |
| Recurring vs one-time (average earning product) | ~$4,053 vs ~$2,047 / month | WhopTrends |
| Base fee | 3% + card processing (2.7% + $0.30) | Sacra / Whop pricing |

Takeaway: most products earn nothing. The ones that do usually run a free product in front of a paid subscription, copy the pricing of whops that already sell, recruit affiliates and bring their own audience.

## Buyer guides

How to judge a whop before paying:

1. Read the member count on the **paid plan card**, not the store total (which includes free members).
2. Read the rating **and** the number of reviews: 4.9 on 20 reviews proves little, 4.8 on 1,500 does.
3. Convert the billing cycle: $14.99 per week is about $780 per year.
4. Read the refund conditions on the product page.
5. Walk away from any guaranteed income claim.

Refunds: open your Whop profile, then Orders, open the purchase and use "Request a refund". If the seller does not answer, contact [Whop support](https://help.whop.com) with the invoice number.
